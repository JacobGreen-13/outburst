Outburst for Debian and Ubuntu ships these components inside /opt/outburst. Each one stays a separate library.

CPython
  License: Python Software Foundation License
  https://docs.python.org/3/license.html
  The interpreter is the official standalone CPython build published by python-build-standalone.
  You may replace /opt/outburst/python with a compatible build.

PySide6 and Qt
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0
  https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html
  The Qt and PySide6 libraries are files under /opt/outburst/python. You may replace those libraries with compatible builds. Qt source is published by The Qt Company.

rawpy and LibRaw
  rawpy license: MIT
  LibRaw license: GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or the Common Development and Distribution License 1.0
  https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
  https://opensource.org/license/cddl-1-0
  LibRaw is the library rawpy uses to read RAW photographs. You may replace that library with a compatible build.

ExifTool
  Copyright (c) Phil Harvey
  License: GNU General Public License v1.0 or later, or the Artistic License (Perl)
  https://exiftool.org/
  ExifTool is a separate program installed by the libimage-exiftool-perl package. Outburst runs it to read capture times. Photographs are passed on the command line and are not uploaded.

This package does not include Ultralytics, PyTorch, or the YOLO weights. The install button in Outburst puts those in the home directory, under ~/.local/share/Outburst/detection, and the model files under ~/.cache/outburst/models.
